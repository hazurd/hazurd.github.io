About

I'm Karl Dagenais — hazurd online. I work hands-on in Kubernetes every day, and I care about the parts of a platform that decide whether deploys land or fail silently: clusters, GitOps with Argo CD, CI/CD pipelines, and observability.

Thirteen years of production infrastructure across insurance, health SaaS, financial markets, and ad-tech. I like security baked in rather than bolted on — least-privilege IAM and RBAC, secrets kept out of plaintext, image scanning, network policies, and alerting that actually means something.
